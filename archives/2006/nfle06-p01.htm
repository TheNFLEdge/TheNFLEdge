The 2006 Pre-season kicks off with a rematch of SB XV, as the legendary Oakland Raiders play to honor the Hall of Fame induction of their former coach, John Madden. SB XV was one of the more memorable moments for the Raider Nation. The deal today is that the Raiders are far removed from the last Raider S.B. team, and well, they pretty much suck. You could have made quite a few bucks were you smart enough to "fade the Raid" with us most of last season! The Eagles have also been down recently so they will be looking to show some thing for the haters who have all picked them to finish last in the NFC east. Well that's all well and good for the regular season, but this is the PRE SEASON and its a WHOLE different game. Unlike some folks who tell you NOT to play the Preseason, the NFL Edge LOVES the pre-season! Ahh, but long time fans know that, and if you ARE one of those (hey WE'RE GLAD YOUR BACK!) or if you checked out our ARCHIVES, you might know we have a soft spot for the Raiders. Well it's a new year, and the Raiders hope to recapture some of that old luster, with Art Shell. No doubt Shell will be looking to resurrect the old Raider mystique and it starts Sunday. It's an open competition for new faces on the Raiders and that could be sloppy play, but usually more effort. Philly has much better depth up front on both lines and should dominate this game. Then again its pre-season and things don't always go like they are planned. Hard core fans of the NFL Edge know some of the angles that PAY during the preseason. The Eagles Mr. Reid is what is known as a Preseason Mush. He won't play the starters very long, and won't show much in the way of plays. The Eagles should roll, were this the regular season, but its preseason, and why not, lets get it out of the way early.... the RAAAAAAAAAYDERZ, and the three points!!
