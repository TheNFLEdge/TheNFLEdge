What looked like it could be a battle for early supremacy in the NFC South, is actually a desperation battle for survival between two teams looking for the 1st win. The Vikes have turned a few heads with a quick start and a solid defense, Brad Childress' purple gang find themselves in a pivotal game for the early lead in the NFC North.
