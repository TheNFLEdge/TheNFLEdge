Thurs. Aug. 21st

Dallas +4.5 vs. Pitts. -4.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Preseason is a proving ground and a tune-up. The first week, after only a week of practice, is just that, practice. The second week, you see some improved play. The third week, as this is, usually is the best indicator of the regular season. This is the week that coaches want to get their starters the most reps and let them go longer. That will make all the games interesting. This one features Parcells' Cowboys - who after getting shut out week 1, rebounded handily pasting Houston last week. Pittsburgh was narrowly edged by a very deep Philly team. Parcells has a great preseason record (47-15 I think) because he plays these games to win. That and the 34 points they put up last week make them AWFULLY tempting! However Pitts starters are just better than the Cowboys and all those tempting reasons to take the Cowboys are exactly why I am going with the Steel!!
