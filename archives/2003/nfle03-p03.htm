Thurs. Aug. 14th

Oakland +2.5 vs. San Fran -2.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

In the NFL they have a saying "your never as good as you are when you win, and never as bad as you look when you lose." Of course that doesn't always apply to the preseason. These "meaningless" games have a whole different take. Since they aren't really played on even footing. The Raiders, a proven veteran team really could care less about this game. You wont see Gannon going to Brown or Rice for TDs, heck they may not even suit up. San Fran will be going without QB Jeff Garcia this week either - as he is out with a bad back. Instead Tim Rattay will man the controls, followed by Brandon Doman and Ken Dorsey. For Doman and Dorsey - they wont be laughing through this game as they are trying to earn a job. SF looked good in their debut winning handily. The Raiders didn't - the PanaGeo Computer - virtually useless in preseason - devouring the stats comes up with SF winning this one. Flaunting in the computers face (or would it be monitor) - I was impressed with the Depth on the Raiders Defense, and especially in these early games when the Defense has the upper hand - I expect they will again. Trying to show that indeed Man is superior to machine - I will put that to the test on the Back of the Silver and Black.
