Baltimore -1.5 at Cincinnati +1.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

A game much tougher than it looks. Any betting maven will tell you Cincy has been a really good "cover" the past few times out, finally breaking through with a win as well. The Ravens have been playing just as well, and folks winning UGLY IS PLAYING WELL. Remember the Raven team that won the Superbowl a few years ago. They won ugly too. This is what Billick does, I have mad respect for him and for what Marvin Lewis is doing up there in Cincy. It will be Jamal Lewis on one side and Ray Lewis on the other and the Ravens getting away with another ugly Win and establishing themselves uglily atop the AFC North.

A huge test for both teams. Minnesota is at 5-0 though some folks still feel they haven't really been tested yet. Denver has been tested by some big games already and last week answered the bell - behind the wily veteran backup QB Steve Buerline. New England +5.5 at Miami -5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

A big early season match up in the AFC East. A Dolphin win would clearly establish the fish in first and give them a huge edge over a Pats team that has rebounded from a rough start and has emerged as the challenger for the AFC East. The Pats won a sloppy game where they played sloppy but were outslopped by their opponent as the Pats created 5 turnovers in that game. The fins have shut people down with defense and some big plays. The key here is this is an early season match up and the fish are really good this time of year. The big burley guys from NE will get worn down in the humidity of Miami in October, and an already depleted defense will tire of tackling big ole Ricky Williams. Ricky will break a big one or two off against a tired defense in the 4th qtr and make a tough defensive struggle look like a blowout in the paper the next day.
************************************
Miami 21 - NE 13

I clearly wrote the Saints off for dead last week, but they rose up and slapped me silly. I had suspected an Aaron Brooks resurgence last week and went against it anyway. Duce McAllister also showed up with perhaps his best rushing performance of the season. Atlanta meanwhile cant feel too good about a 36-0 slap down in St Louis. Philadelphia +3.5 at N.Y. Giants -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Giants have looked dominant at times, and still they lose the game. They did this in both their last two losses, driving the ball and coughing it up. That is really starting to play on their minds. The Eagles who seemed to have righted their ship had things backfire on them in Big D. From the opening kickoff - a trick play gone awry, to the last play of a rallying drive to steal victory derailed by a big sack. Hard to tell what to expect from either team this week. This much I know the G-men always struggle against scrambling QBs. If you aren't Kurt Warner and go down from the rush of wind created when they rush past you, missing tackles. The more agile QBs escape, and always kill the G-men with the big scramble on a big pass rush. McNabb is the master of that and if Andy Reid lets him roll out and scramble away out of the pocket it will be a very long day for the G-mens D-men and their fans.
************************************
Philly 30 - Giants 20
************************************
 FINAL PHI 14 - NYG 10 WINNER  

San Diego +5.5 at Cleveland -5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Bolts go to Brownland to take on the resurgent Clevelanders. Controversy or not with Tim Couch at the helm the Brownlanders have won their last two. The Bolts are a tough luck bunch - eventually they can win a game like this one that no one suspects. However with that Defense they are bringing to Cleveland RB Green will get his 100 yards, Couch will connect on some TDs and check out the Browns record when Green goes over 100 - yup it usually means a victory.
