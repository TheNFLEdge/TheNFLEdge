Seattle +2.5 at San Francisco -2.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

In an interesting turn of events Mike Holmgren and his Seahawks come calling on a San Fran team playing out the string. Seattle desperately needing a road win to keep alive a chance for the post season. The Hawks weren't intimidated last time out in St Louis and wont be intimidated this time either. The Niners may be playing out the string, but shown the league that they haven't quit yet beating Philly in Philly last week with a title on the line for Philly. San Fran would be a dangerous dog here and rightly should be favored(there is no line as of this writing - except my guess in parens), but without their main man WR Terrell Owens out with an injury, the desperate Hawks, will finally get that elusive road W, but will it be enough to get them to the postseason?
************************************
Seattle 31 - San Fran 30
************************************

Sunday, Dec. 28

Chicago +10.5 at Kansas City -10.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Da Bears, come into KC eyeballing an 8-8 finish to a season that started abysmally. Instead its KC who looks pretty shaken heading into the playoffs. They shown everyone they couldn't stop the run last week, but giving up 45 points they looked like they couldn't stop anything. The Bears will definitely test that suspect defense with A-train back and running behind that O-line. The Bears will try and grind it out on them. Realistically though a Rook QB like Rex Grossman, in his first road start in Arrowhead, even if he had Jim Brown in his prime behind him to hand the ball to, would have trouble beating the Chiefs there this week.
************************************
KC 30 - Chi 19
***********************************
FINAL KC 31 - Chi 3 WINNER  

Cleveland +7.5 at Cincinnati -7.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The season finale of the Ohio Rivalry! The Browns are pretty much playing out the string. While Cincy still has playoff hopes dancing in their heads. Cleveland could salvage their season by spoiling Cincys. Lets call this game the parody bowl shall we. I know its hard in this MTV generation of instant gratification, but recall back last year when the roles were reversed and Cleveland snuck into the playoffs on a victory in the last weekend (with a barrage of other happenings). Butch Davis was being hailed as a bright young coach. What difference a year makes. Now Cincy is the one playing that last week and hoping for outside help. They will do their part as Cleveland cant even slow a good running game down, let alone stop it.
************************************
Cincy 27 - Cle. 10
************************************
FINAL Cle 22 - Cin 14 Loser  

Dallas -1.5 at New Orleans +1.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Cowpokes wrapped up a playoff spot and with some help from San Fran are eyeing the NFC East crown. New Orleans eliminated themselves with a lackluster effort in Jacksonville. Despite the ridiculous play at the end - they blew the extra point and any post season hopes. How will they get up for this game? They wont, and the Cowboys will light them up. They wont be pulling Cell phones out this week, especially since Joe Horn dislocated his shoulder. Let that be an importance Karmic lesson to you youngsters, when you try and show someone up - sure it seems funny at the time - but it WILL come back to you later someway. Watch Roy Williams pull out a phony Arm Sling after he dislodges some body part from someone. See what I mean?
************************************
Dallas 23 - NO 20
************************************
FINAL NO 13 - Dal 7 Losers  

Indianapolis -6.5 at Houston +6.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Colts still have a shot at home field and definitely don't want to slip down to a 3 seed or even a wildcard. So they will definitely show up this week. Houston wont give an inch to anyone. Contesting every yard and every point with a respectable defensive unit. It wont be enough against a determined Colt team - but those Texans, from their efforts throughout this season have finally earned some respect.
************************************
Indy 27 - Hous. 16
************************************
FINAL Indy 20 - Hou 17 WINNER/No Cover

Jacksonville +3.5 at Atlanta -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Well I was one week too early looking for that Micheal (Vick) Magic! I knew he had a big upset in him, as they are a completely different team with him at the helm, I just called it one week too soon. It will be a taller task this time as they tangle with Mr Leftwich and his Jags. The Jags are hot featuring one of the best run defenses in the league. They will need all of that to contain Mr. Vick, and some big plays from the "other" QB in this game. That might be too much to ask from a young team on the road like that - but I gotta go with the team that has been good to me - at least with the points - just one more time!
