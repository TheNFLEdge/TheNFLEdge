Saturday, Dec. 20

Atlanta +6.5 at Tampa Bay -6.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

After the pasting in Indy the Falcons fly down to Tampa to take more punishment from the Tampa defense. The Champs need this game to stay in the postseason hunt. More of the same for Atlanta this week as the Bucs were the inspiration for Tony Dungys defensive game plan which stifled Micheal Vick last week. The Bucs have better personnel on defense to play that scheme and contain Vick. Once they do that Atlanta really has no shot.

Kansas City -3.5 at Minnesota +3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Vikes are also now in a struggle for a playoff spot. They have faded from their 6-0 start and are now tied with Green Bay. Fortunately for them the Chiefs come in with the AFC west all wrapped up. Still the Chiefs want to capture home field if they have any shot at the Super Bowl. So this will be a huge game. Minnesota is lethal at home - but their defense is very generous. The Chiefs are also generous against the run and they have no one to take away Randy Moss so I am going purple here gang!
************************************
MINNY 33 - K.C. 31

 FINAL MINN 45 - KC 20 WINNER  

New England -4.5 at N.Y. Jets +4.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Sunday, Dec. 21

Baltimore -3.5 at Cleveland +3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Nothing is decided yet in the AFC North. Both Balt and Cincy sit tied atop this division. Despite all Baltimore has accomplished, and the impressive victory over Cincy the past week, it could all be for naught if they lose this game. Don't kid yourself one bit about Cleveland. They may not be that good, but they are still fighting hard, showing all wise guys that they weren't 10 points worse than Denver as Mr. Bomber warned me. They will get run on and over this week though.
************************************
Balt. 27 - Cle 20
***********************************
 FINAL BALT 35 - CLE 0 WINNER  

Cincinnati +6.5 at St. Louis -6.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

A huge game for both these two. St Louis knows the importance of the Dome field advantage in the playoffs. While Cincy is actually thinking post season - and a win here would really be a big stride in that direction. Despite the great job Marvin Lewis has done this year, he will struggle to find answers for the Rams in the Dome. Cincy fans don't fret though this was a heck of a season for ya, and even a loss does not eliminate Cincy this week. If you told any Bengal fan that they would go into week 17 with a chance to make the playoffs - they would take that. They will be taking a lot more this Sunday though!
************************************
St Lou 37 - Cincy 30

Detroit +9.5 at Carolina -9.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

"One of our goals this season was to close the gap in our division" - John Fox, Coach of the Panthers. Hey John, I would say you accomplished that, winning the NFC South. I have been on Carolina most this season, and this week will be no different. I am worried though that with the winning the division, the Cats really have nothing to play for now. For them home field is out, and they may not be playing hard. The solace for me here is that Detroit has been playing that way all season except the one "excuse me" game on Thanksgiving!

 FINAL CAR 20 - DET 14 WINNER/No Cover  

Miami +1.5 at Buffalo -1.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

A strange game to handicap actually. Miami is terrible in December, and much worse in the lousy weather. Make no bones about it Buffalo this time of year is nasty. Well the weather certainly is, The Bills though really aren't. They have been playing hard, and at home in December, they are extra tough. Greg Williams may be a lame duck coach though, and he may not really have his guys playing hard for him in this one. Miami may also be in the dumps after the Monday night beat down at home, all their December failings are no doubt in their mind. Its those ugly green shirt-green pants unis. though! Don't they know that looks awful! Break out with the orange jerseys and shock the Bills!! They wont though, cause Wannstedt is just as bad a coach as Williams. The Fins will continue their December swoon, which they have mentally already resigned to.
************************************
Buff 20 - Mia 19
************************************
 FINAL MIA 20 - BUF 3 COVER  

New Orleans at Jacksonville E

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The smoking saints scorched NY Sunday night. While the red hot Jags were cooled off in a snowy New England. This will be a big time match up as both these two are playing great ball. Hard to figure what Saints team will come out. If they click like they did on Sunday night it will be hard for the Jags to stop them. Then again the Saints Defense has been very porous all year, and going against an offense with NFL caliber offensive linemen may expose that. The Jags let me down last week but I can excuse one loss in the Snow. I don't know if I can excuse the Saints.
************************************
Jax 23 - N.O. 21
