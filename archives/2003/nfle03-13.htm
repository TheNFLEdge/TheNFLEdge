Thursday, Nov. 27

Green Bay -6.5 at Detroit +6.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Detroit is showcased as this years Thanksgiving day lead off game. Traditionally the Lions have been pretty good at home on Thanksgiving. With the whole country watching this would be a great time for Mariucci to earn tons of respect, and QB Joey Harrington to have a break out game. As the underdog Lions have done Thanksgivings past! Then again this is a pretty good Green Bay team, which is actually in a tight race for the NFC North division. A national game too, which means the Pack couldn't possibly underestimate the Lions. Farve doesn't play well in domes, and the points make Detroit pretty inviting. Thinking about it though, lets say it does get into the 4th qtr, and it's a real close game, 2 minutes left. My money goes on the man who has proven he can get it done in those situations, not the kid who still needs to show me, and everyone - might this be the passing of the torch... nah.

GB 27 - DET 20

Miami +3.5 at Dallas -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Crack in their Armor?? Who said that??? Well whomever did The Cowboys respectfully disagree. Parcells is the master, throwing the option play at Carolina last week, and hanging on to the top spot in the East as well as the whole NFC. Got to hand it to the man. Miami on the other hand comes into this game with a solid record, yet all the talk still in Florida is how DISAPPOINTING the Dolphins have been? Hey winning ugly is still winning in my book. Even though Jay Fiedler is a pretty ugly QB and doesn't play real "pretty" he does win lots of games. Games like this, "heavyweight" match ups, Miami shows up to play - as long as the weather doesn't get that cold. Then again last time there was a Thanksgiving day game in Dallas and it snowed, Miami won. Will they be wearing orange for this one??

Miami 19 - Dall 17

 FINAL MIA 40 - DAL 21 WINNER

Atlanta +3.5 at Houston -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Those wily Texans. A great sleeper throughout most of this season they finally get to wear the respect of a favorite. Can they "texan-up" and win this game. Sure is possible. Atlanta might be the worst team in the league sure. Vick is not back yet, and they just lost leading rusher Warrick Dunn to the IR. So why take them. Because despite getting beaten again last week, they are playing improved ball, having lead most of the Tennessee game. They do have the capability of playing solid defense. Houston has some nice offense but are they good enough to go out and win this game. Probably, still despite the long odds, I like the Falcons to have one of their biggest defensive efforts, lots of RB TJ Duckett, some good breaks, and come home with the upset victory.
************************************
Altanta 24 - Hou. 23
***********************************
 FINAL HOU 17 - ATL 13 Loser  

Buffalo +2.5 at N.Y. Giants -2.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Bills visit the Gmen in a battle between similar disappointing teams. Both had high hopes for this season, and both see their season all but over. It will be interesting to note which team comes out and plays harder. The Bills have had trouble moving the ball, and could have trouble again in NY. While the Giants have moved the ball but have trouble scoring. This one is all about coaching though, as the better coach will keep his team focused on this one game and not dwelling on the records. Fassell and the Giants have a way of rising up this time of year, throw in that the burden of being in the playoff race has been lifted, and the Giants can get back to playing decent football.
************************************
Giants 27 - Buff 10
************************************
 FINAL BUF 24 - NYG 7 Loser

Cincinnati +2.5 at Pittsburgh -2.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Bengals, like it or not are one of - if not THE hottest team in the NFL right now. They just got through spanking San Diego and staying on top in the AFC North division. The Steelers come in behind Cincy, in the standings, but feeling they are the better team. This is actually a tough game to call as the Cincy really has been playing very well. Pittsburgh has shown that they are vulnerable. Despite the improved play of Cincy, and its led by their defense, they are still young. Pittsburgh might not be lighting it up, but they are still scoring. Maddox will find some holes in the Cincy secondary. The maligned Pittsburgh defense will contain Kitna and the surprising Bengals will get a dose of the "any given Sunday blues"
************************************
Pitt 31 - Cincy 20
************************************
 FINAL CINCY 24 - PITT 20 Loser  

Minnesota +5.5 at St. Louis -5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Vikings come into St Louis off a big victory at home over a game Detroit team. The Rams struggled at home with Arizona last week and let them climb back into the game and needed overtime to pull it out. The Vikes will no doubt test the St Louis defense. I really think that St Louis has a very good defensive team and will find answers to the Vikes attack. Offensively they can and will exploit some things as Marshall Faulk is getting more reps with the team and should make some things happen. The only edge I like is Mike Tice over Mike Martz. Martz has more talent on Defense but is a buffoon at times. Tice deserves to win this game -- which is why Martzes guys will.

New England +4.5 at Indianapolis -4.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Big time match up of two big time AFC Powers! This is a huge game for both teams but ultimately I think this particular game means more to Indy. They really think that this game will be a huge factor in their postseason hopes, and will play like it's a big pressure game. New Eng. Is looking at this game as a huge test - but they will be business as usual - yet even if they come up short they know that there is still much more football to be played. The Pats have been there, they will be more relaxed they know this game DON'T meant the super bowl or not. The Colts will feel the pressure, when Brady and the Opportunistic Pats will come out firing - how will they respond? This round will go to the home team, but this wont be the last we hear from these two...
