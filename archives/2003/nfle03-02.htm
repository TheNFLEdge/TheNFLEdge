This is wicked game any time these two get together. Miami was more than likely looking past the Texans, and will be much more focused on this game. Still other than Ricky Williams what have the Dolphins got? Chris Chambers had a nice game - but Jay Fiedlers the QB and his critical pick in the fourth did them in. The Jets know the Phins - in fact they sort of own the Phins - aside from last year they had beaten them some nine in a row. The Jets will play hard and tough at home. Pittsburgh +3.5 at Kansas City -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

A tough tough game. Kansas City looks pretty darn good at home and the Steelers with Tommy Maddox lit up the Ravens last week. Sure that was in Pittsburgh and Kansas City is a real tough place to go into and play. The Steelers could have one of the best WR corps in the league, and Maddox looks real good throwing to them. I might be wrong picking this upset - but I will take Maddox and the points in what looks to be a shootout.
