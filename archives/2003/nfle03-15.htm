Buffalo +5.5 at Tennessee -5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The recently resurgent Bills - winners of two in a row, come down to visit the suddenly struggling Titans who have dropped their last two. Don't know that this is exactly the cause to panic for Tennessee fans - but Steve McNairs bone spur might be. Again he is questionable for this weeks game. This does not bode well for a team with post season aspirations. Nor does it bode well if they keep getting their QB beaten down week in and week out. The Titans should right the ship this week, as Buffalo really struggles on the road, even if McNair does not play.
************************************
Tenn 26 - Buff 13

Dallas -1.5 at Washington +1.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The old time shoot out between these classic rivals. Dallas comes in on a bit of a slide having lost two in a row and 3 of their last 4. The Redskins meanwhile posted a very impressive road win up in New York, and more importantly have covered the point spread in 4 out of their last 5 games. This means that the Skins arent just playing out the string, they are still fighting hard. Dallas is also fighting, fighting for a playoff spot, and despite the setbacks. Parcells will find places to exploit the exploitable Skins and will Pressure young backup QB Tim Hasselbeck into mistakes which will prove the difference in this NFC Eastern showdown.
************************************
Dallas 23 - Was. 20
