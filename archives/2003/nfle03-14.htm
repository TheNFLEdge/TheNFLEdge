Chicago +5.5 at Green Bay -5.5

Hey don't look now, but the Bears have a bit of a win streak going! Believe this or not but the Bears have played their way back into the playoff picture. Back in Pack-land, outdoors in the cold where Bretts hands are all numb from the cold and he cant feel his broken thumb as he will lead the pack to a vengeful victory throttling the upstart Chicagoans.

Cincinnati +3.5 at Baltimore -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Another enormous D-DAY match up. The stunning, amazing, first place Bengals visit the Baltimore badlands patrolled by Ray Lewis. What was that I was saying when I said momentum can take a team a long way. 44 points coming off another 40 point effort you might think Baltimore was an offensive juggernaut. Yet they create all those points with their Defense! Having a truck like Jamal Lewis helps too. Wait now the Bengals are built in that same mold, a solid defense molded by Marvin Lewis. (hey that's a Lewis trifecta) with an offensive line moving people out of the way for the likes of Rudi Johnson and Coery Dillon (what no Lewis??) - this will be a big time hitting game. Cincy though don't have anyone like "Sugar" Ray Lewis on their defense. Sugar Ray is the most dominant defender in the NFL and will show you Sunday.

Hey now here's a game that would have surprised me if you told me would be between two teams playing out the string. That's what it is though.
