The final pre-season game is usually the worst to bet on. Most teams like Pitts. know who the starting QB, RB and other guys will be and usually don't even dress them for these games. All that being said it's the battle of the back ups. Carolina is still looking at getting guys in synch and they have had a really good pre-season. The Steelers have had a mixed preseason winning games but playing and looking at lots of guys. Maddox likely wont play this game, which means lots of Charlie Batch. Against a really good Carolina Defense that really excites me - even if it is pre-season. The one worry I have is that St. Pierre the 3rd stringer comes in the second half and leads the Steelers back. I say he will but it wont be enough as Carolina will have it all wrapped up by then.
