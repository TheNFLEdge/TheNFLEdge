Aug. 7th

NY Giants +3.5 vs. N.E. Patriots -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Now I might be tipping my hat a little bit here, especially in light of the fact that our Pre-Season Spectacular is not due out for at least another week or two. However at risk of some of you not coming back for the pre-season spectacular, which lets face it NO ONE IN THEIR RIGHT MIND WOULD DO, we will tip our hand a little. We expect BIG things from both these two this year. The Giants ended last season with a bitter taste in their mouths as the refs blew the call at the end of the playoff game and sent the G-men into the offseason. By all accounts the G-men are chomping to get back on the field and I expect a big effort even in the pre-season. As this is the first action for them since that game. The Pats on the other side of the ball are a year removed from Super Bowl Champions and didn't even qualify for the playoffs last year. They are equally primed to show the home fans that the Super Bowl run was no fluke. As it's the first pre-season game though, and these two will be playing in the regular season anyway - I don't expect to see too much from either team. A series or two from the starters at most. The Pats will be taking a close look at QB Rohan Davey, an athletic QB who can scramble and give the Giants D fits. The Giants have a couple of guys who will be playing hard to show they belong in the NFL - and one of them is former Heisman Trophy winner Ron Dayne. He will be featured in this game, and determined to have a big day. Playing against second teamers he just might.
