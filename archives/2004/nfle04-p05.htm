Texas A&M +8.5 at Utah -8.5

Two great coaches go head to head in TAMU's Dennis Franchoine and Utah's Urban Meyer. The Doc sees this chess match being won by the upstart Utes at home. Oregon State +17.5 at LSU -17.5

GET THE LIVE COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING LINES
This LSU team is for real, but they will be tested by one of the better defenses in the nation this week. The Doc sees this as a statement game for the Bayou Bengals...the team has stated that they feel like the forgotten Champ from last season, with USC garnering all the preseason hype. This is a chance for LSU to show te country that they are the best team in the land with a strong showing against a quality opponent. Toledo +11.5 at Minnesota -11.5

GET THE LIVE COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING LINES
Yes, Toledo is a MAC team, but haven't y'all been paying attention? The MAC has been playing very well for the past couple of years and this Toledo squad is a good one. Led by QB Bruce Gradkowski, they can eat up yards and pile up points against anyone. The problem is, they really don't have much of a defense, and that can hurt against a power running team like the Gophers. Look for Minnesota to try to control the clock with the ground attack and beat the Rockets soundly.

Syracuse +12.5 at Purdue -12.5

GET THE LIVE COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING LINES
Purdue coach Joe Tiller lost a ton of talent from last year's bunch, but QB Kyle Orton returns to lead Tiller's prolific attack. The 'Cuse still hasn't come up with a quarterback to lead the offense, so Walter Reyes will have to be the star for now.
