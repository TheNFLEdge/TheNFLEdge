Cincinnati at New England -11.5

GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW!

Should be one of the most interesting match ups in the AFC. New England starting to look like they are getting that late season roll on. Along comes the Bengals. Winners of 2 in a row, 4 of the last 5 and having beaten the spread 5 weeks in a row. Sure the X-treme Stats show the Bengals are 3-7 in its last 10 December games. And a paltry 2-4 ATS record on the road, but they already tallied one big road win last week. Statistically it would seem the odds are stacked against that happening twice in a row. The Cocky Bengals though don't seem to care though. They have been on fire down the stretch and could wind up upsetting the Pats. Im not going out on that far with my pick, but I do like the Bengals to exploit N.E.s suspect corners with Carson Palmer throwing to Chad Johnson as this one is not the lock it may seem like.

Defense still decides games and despite all the offense, the Vikes have the edge on Defense in this one, and that will be the key.

Miami at Denver -11.5

GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW!

Here is what might appear to be another lopsided easy pick, but might not be in reality. I know all about A.J. Feeley and his five picks. That was pretty bad, if you take away the picks and the points there you can clearly see Miami as a team has not quit. Hard to overcome 5 Interceptions (and two more fumbles to boot) but its also hard to deny 32 points being scored and a defense that was still managing to get some turnovers and fight hard. Denver is still alive for a playoff and need a win badly, but Im still not sold on them. QB Jake Plummer though might not want to be outdone by Mr Feeley and could toss 6 picks of his own this week. The Dolphins will fight but in the end they will falter again. Them Florida Fish don't do too well late in the year and in cold even when they aren't this bad.

Denver 27 - Miami 20
************************************

St. Louis at Carolina -4.5

St. Louis found their way back into the lead in the NFC West, but shouldn't be too comfortable with a 6-6 mark.
