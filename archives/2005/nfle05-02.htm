GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW!

Here is another of the great rivalry games, these two don’t like each other, and that makes for some great games. This series is always fun to watch. Although to admit lately it’s been pretty one sided in favor of New York. This season the Dolphins sport a new swagger, having just routed Denver. While the Jets are reeling from a pitiful performance in Kansas City in week one. They will be looking to turn it around this week. Miami has some decent weapons at WR - they can make plays for them. The Dolphins still have a really good defense, but lets face it offensively they aren’t quite the scoring machine Kansas City is. The Jets defense should be able to rebound with a solid effort this week. The X factor in this game will be New York’s offense. If they can step it up and eliminate the mistakes, they can turn it around. If however they let Miami’s defense dictate to them, they will be in for a long day, and a long season. I think Pennington gets it on track at home this week, and plays much better. Miami will give them a heck of a game, and I think that line may be a little too generous for suddenly confident Dolphin "D". However in the end when I have to lay the lettuce, I don’t know if I can see the Dolphins having another big day against the Jets defense. Their offense is too suspect for me to see them go into New York and have a big day.
