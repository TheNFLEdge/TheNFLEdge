Atlanta at Tampa Bay -3.5

GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW! * EXTREME NFL MATCH UPS!

At this stage in the NFL season, what you have are essentially playoff games, and games just like the preseason, meaningless but they still have to play them. This one is one of the former, Atlanta needs this game as they are in a dogfight for a playoff spot. Likewise Tampa was demoralized in New England last week. The recent history shows a HUGE edge for the Bucs having won and covered in 8 of the last 10 meetings with the Falcons. Kind of a long shot in this one, but I got a hunch Mr. Vick has one big effort left in him, and the Falcons get a little revenge over the Buckling Bucs.

GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW! * EXTREME NFL MATCH UPS!

The Bengals are enjoying the happiest of Holidays in southern Ohio in many a year. The Bengals have clinched the AFC North, and going to the playoffs. That makes this game much less like an NFL game, and certainly wont be a playoff atmosphere. Instead it will be more like a preseason game, with a Party. A very cold preseason game, and a very festive holiday party. Now you know what happens at Holiday Parties this time of year, thats right someone gets too drunk and strange things happen. Just like that, this game has almost no meaning, as the Bengals have the division secure and wont get the no. 1 seed with the Colts having that already wrapped up. So that makes this essentially a preseason game. Preseason games by rule are risky to bet on. So is this one. The Bengals may be treating this like a party, or they could be focused determined to send a message and have another big comfortable win. See this is where all that preseason wagering pays off. Remember way back to August, how The Bengals were crushing people even in those meaningless games. Even on cruise control the Bengals should cruise against the limping Bills.
