NY GIANTS at NY JETS -6.5

GET THE LIVE NFL BETTING LINE NOW!

The Jets host the Giants in the battle of New York. How much BIGGER CAN IT GET!?! The NYers have been scrapping since they practiced together up in Albany two weeks ago. The Giants accused the Jets of taking some cheap shots in PRACTICE! The Jets were insinuating the Giants were soft for whinning about "real" hitting. This game will be bigger in the media than it is on the field. Sure the Jets are a big favorite due to the "news" that NY Giant QB Eli Manning will be missing the game. However look for a rookie running back named Brandon Jacobs for the Giants to be a big load in the second half against the second stringers. The Jets will race off to an early lead, then Jacobs will get going and run all over the Jets defense behind some depth on the Giants offensive line, and look for the Giants to control the second half.
