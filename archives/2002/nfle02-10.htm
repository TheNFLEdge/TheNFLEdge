Atlanta +4.5 at Pittsburgh -4.5

Remember who told y'all not to fall in love with Atlanta. Spotting Baltimore 7.5 last week was a big loser. Meanwhile I have been anything but in love with Pittsburgh this year. Well after picking against them for like 4 straight weeks I finally, grudgingly admit that Pitts is a good team. They arent that good that they cant be upset by Atlanta - but in thier division they can lose this game and still be in first. Always tough to go against Micheal Vick, but I think the Steelers will borrow a page from the Ravens and bottle up Slick Vick, leaving QB Tommy Maddox to mystify me with his aerial magic.

Baltimore is no expansion team but in thier past two they have sure played like one. They should be up for a visit from the Bengals. Cincy does not have as good a Defense as Baltimore does, and more often than not, that will decide games - this one should be no exception.
