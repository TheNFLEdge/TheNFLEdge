Dallas +10.5 at St. Louis -10.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Remarkable what a different team the Rams are with and without RB Marshall Faulk. Without him the St. Louis offense looked pathetic against Tampa. With him they went 91 yds in 3 minutes for the TD almost effortlessly. So that has a huge bearing on this game and the number. I figure it should be at about 7 without him, maybe 14 to 17 with him. Dallas was humiliated in Philly and now gets to travel to St Louis to face a team looking for its first win. Dallas will need a huge defensive game - having to get to Warner, like the Bucs did, to do anything and a solid effort from thier QB Troy Ai... oh... thats right he retired. Well even if he wasn't it wouldn't matter this week, just by how much???

Cleveland +5.5 at Pittsburgh -5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

Pittsburgh is another winless team who many picked to be in the SuperBowl. Struggling against the Pass -- Cleveland will probably come out throwing. Do you know how to fix that? Running the ball. Long time consuming drives, that end with touchdowns. The Steelers could and should 'take the air out of the ball' as Bill Parcells used to say. Grind the ball with Bettis, QB draws with Stewart, avoid punting to Denis Northcutt, and hope Couch's recievers have the dropsies, or get this - actually TRY and cover someone, yup if ALL that happens, Pittsburgh will finally get a win. I for one am not ready to write Pittsburgh off... just yet.

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The only thing more ridiculous than this point spread is calling this a game. Philly is spotting Houston 20 POINTS - are you serious. Is this Houston the pro team or the University of Houston. I mean its ludicrous to expect a pro team not to cover a 20 point spread. Although anything is possible, like Philly getting out front early then stick in the JV squad and Houston could rally to cover. That would be the only possibility for drama, and the way Philly is playing it would be safer to lay the 20.

Miami -5.5 at Kansas City +5.5

GET THE LIVE NFL LINES NOW!

The Dolphins come in as confident as anyone - especially after ridding themselves of the Jets Jinx. Oh yeah and in addition they are looking as good as anyone right now. Ricky Williams really brings a whole bunch to this offense, he is probably looking as good as he has as a pro. They should wear down the Chiefs defense. The Chiefs will get their points and they have a decent back of thier own with Priest Holmes, who may jump out and surprise the Fish early. The Phins may be in for that 'post big game' flat syndrome -- see the Giants game last week home vs Seattle. However its still September, and that means the Phins will find a way to overcome a tight game in a hostile place.
