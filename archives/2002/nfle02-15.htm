Baltimore -2.5 at Houston +2.5

The upstart Texans had a very impressive win in Pitt last week. Upon further review they scored 3 TDs off returns - fumbles, ints. Not taking anything away from the Texans who have won 2 of their last 3 games because of that opportunistic defense, but you have to wonder where they would be without their opponents making big mistakes. Baltimore had a tough one last week, but overall have been suprisingly solid this year. If they dont make big mistakes offensively and keep getting the ball to RB Jamal Lewis they should win this game.
